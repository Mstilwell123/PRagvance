# pragvance.ai AI Spin Up page PRD (draft, Nov 21 2026)

Outcome: in 3 seconds a pragvance.ai visitor understands AI Spin Up (an AI agent in the owner's voice that talks with prospects and books calls) and taps one button to talk to Elaine live.

## Ledger

- R1 Single H1 'AI Spin Up: talk to an AI Spin Up agent.' at the top of the hero, readable on the first mobile screen. MUST
- R2 The tagline 'Practical AI. Built to advance your business.' sits directly under the H1, verbatim. MUST
- R3 A primary CTA 'Talk to an AI Spin Up agent' links to https://connect-now-landing.vercel.app/, with the URL kept in one constant. MUST
- R4 pragvance.ai header and footer with the PRAGVANCE.AI text wordmark, and no second logo image. MUST
- R5 Explain the agent in the owner's voice, the 5-10 minute interview, the service-area boundary, and the four steps. MUST
- R6 The summary email, missed-call text and weekly digest are labeled 'coming soon'. MUST
- R7 No AI Spin Up pricing; a 'See plans' link goes to the AI Spin Up site. MUST
- R8 Zero changes to the Done-for-You card or /done-for-you, and no redirects to them. MUST
- R9 The page lives at /ai-spin-up, and /spin-up, /hi and /talk-to-an-ai-agent redirect to it with a 308. MUST
- R10 Unique title and meta, self-referencing canonical, OG and Twitter tags, and an OG image. MUST
- R11 Valid JSON-LD for Organization, WebPage, SoftwareApplication or Product (no ratings), FAQPage and BreadcrumbList. MUST
- R12 An FAQ of 5-7 Q&As written for answer engines, matching the FAQPage schema. MUST
- R13 The page is in the sitemap, allowed in robots, and has an AI Spin Up section in llms.txt. MUST
- R14 Keywords come from real cited sources, with no invented volumes, and are used in the route, title, meta, headings and schema. MUST
- R15 Good Core Web Vitals: image dimensions set, no layout shift, minimal JS, and Lighthouse results reported. MUST
- R16 A Vercel branch preview URL is reported, with production and the domain unchanged. MUST
- R17 Design first: type scale, spacing, hierarchy, mobile-first, with desktop and mobile screenshots. MUST

## Non-goals

No merge, no production deploy, no domain or DNS changes, no pricing, no Done-for-You changes, never the word 'swarm', no fake metrics or testimonials, no restructuring of unrelated pages.
